About

Game developer with 5 years of professional experience in Unity and C# across five studios. I started out shipping hyper-casual, hybrid and idle mobile games, and now work on PC: at Old Stuff Studio I built most of the systems behind Tattoo Shop Simulator, released on Steam in 2026. Before that, I always worked in cross-functional teams of three or more, alongside game designers and 3D artists.

I focus on maintainable, scalable game systems: clean architecture with dependency injection and event-driven design, save data that survives live updates, and performance-conscious code on hot paths. Alongside mobile titles with monetization and analytics integration, I have taken a PC game from development to Steam release, including platform integration and post-launch support, and I build Unity editor tooling and build automation to streamline development.
